## 5. Must / ambition / deferred / never (proposed)

1. Personal shelf separate from org SOP store.  
2. Create/edit at least one personal `.md` or skill.  
3. Load personal content as **T3**; cannot override Never/HITL/grants.  
4. H6 required for org promotion; no auto-promote.  
5. Block `compatibility: internet` skills.  
6. Not weight training.  
7. Honesty: **User feature**, not PS-mandated “self-learning.”

### Org-ambition

- Assisted “capture from this task” drafts; search personal library; bulk H6 queue.

### Deferred

- Sync across devices; ML clustering of habits.

### Never

- Unsupervised weight training on plant data.  
- Auto-promote to org.  
- Personal `.md` as ACL/grant store.  
- WAN skill hub install.  
- Store personal files as if they were controlled SOPs.
