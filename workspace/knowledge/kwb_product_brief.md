## Apex Refining · Knowledge Work Bench (KWB) — Product brief

**Owner:** Digital Knowledge Office (synthetic)  
**Audience:** Inspectors, integrity engineers, desk operators  
**Status:** Internal brief · SIH26 demo corpus

### What KWB is
Knowledge Work Bench is an **offline industrial evidence and document workbench**.
It helps staff find, reconcile, and explain evidence; run transparent sandbox calcs;
and draft Office deliverables (Word / PowerPoint) while preserving an approval trail.

### What KWB is not
- Not a chatbot for plant control
- Not an autonomous refinery operator
- Not a safety, legal, financial, or statutory decision-maker
- Never writes to DCS / SIS / PLC / EAM / ERP / permits
