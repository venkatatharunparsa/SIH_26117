## 1. On-slide paste

### Target audience

| Who | What changes |
|---|---|
| **Primary:** inspection / integrity engineer | Guided path to **DRAFT Word** from extract; **they** approve |
| **Secondary:** process, HSE, maintenance | Same workbench; more skills Later |
| **Org (MRPL-class):** 15.0 MMTPA CPSE · ~2,530 staff | Reduce shadow paste of P&IDs; reuse mid-range GPU |
| **Replication:** other PSUs / defence-linked plants | Same `/v1` socket; plant-specific templates |

### Benefits (one line each)

- **Social:** Reduce incentive to paste confidential drawings into public GenAI (**48%** of orgs admit pasting non-public data — Cisco Privacy Benchmark 2024; **not** an MRPL survey).  
- **Economic:** Cut elapsed **find-and-type** time; reuse GPUs already purchased. **No ₹crore plant-savings claim.**  
- **Environmental:** No extra training run; no cloud round-trip of P&IDs (do **not** claim “greener than cloud”).  
- **Governance:** On-prem prompts/logs + audit trail — **controls**, not a legal certification.

### KPI (print exactly this one)

**Time (preferred):**  
Scanned inspection pack today **~1–5 days** elapsed → **target 2–3 hours to a DRAFT**  
*Industry workflow study — **estimate, not an MRPL SLA**; engineer still approves; not FFS / shutdown authority.*

### Optional micro-proof (footer)

Working prototype: fail-closed gates · Electron desk · export leave soft copy.
