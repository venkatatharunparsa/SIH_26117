# Non-Technical Brief — Knowledge Work Bench

**For:** Org mentors, non-CS teammates, pitch practice  
**Product name:** Knowledge Work Bench (KWB)

---

## In one sentence

A **private desk** that helps engineers turn **inspection scans and notes** into a **Word draft for approval**, without sending confidential plant data to public AI chatbots.

---

## Who it is for

People in **refineries, PSUs, defence manufacturing, and government offices** who today either:
- type approval notes by hand for days, or  
- risk pasting sensitive text into ChatGPT / Claude.

---

## What it does (plain)

1. You open a folder of files (scan, PDF, notes).  
2. The assistant **reads** them (OCR / PDF text).  
3. It shows you the facts and asks: **is this correct?**  
4. It looks up the **company SOP** on your machine — not on the internet.  
5. It writes a **Word draft**. You check it.  
6. You take a **leave pack** to the human Approver. The app does **not** approve for the plant.

There is also a **coding** mode for small calculations in a guarded sandbox.

---

## Why this is not “just another ChatGPT”

| ChatGPT / Claude | Knowledge Work Bench |
|---|---|
| Data goes to a vendor cloud | Models run on your LAN / workstation |
| Answers may invent numbers | Designed to **cite or say not found** |
| Feels like chat | Feels like an **inspection workflow with gates** |
| Can feel like the decision-maker | **Human keeps judgement** |

---

## What success looks like on stage

- Show two model choices for two jobs (inspect vs code).  
- Show scan → Word draft.  
- Show Monitor: “we are talking to our own machine / peer — not OpenAI.”  
- Say clearly: draft ≠ approved plant record.

---

## Words to avoid

- “Fully air-gapped” (unless you show the network proof)  
- “CERT certified”  
- “Autonomous plant operator”  
- “Same as Cursor”

---

## Words to use

- On-prem / private  
- Human-in-the-loop  
- Soft-copy leave  
- Cite or abstain  
- Sovereign workbench for confidential knowledge work
