# Master narrative — one story, six batons

**Product:** Knowledge Work Bench (KWB)  
**PS:** SIH26117 · MRPL · Smart Automation · Software  
**Audience:** Tired remote screener (~2 min) then live jury  

---

## The story in one breath (memorise)

Industrial sites cannot send P&IDs, inspection packs, or board drafts to Claude or Codex. Engineers either assemble notes by hand for days, or risk shadow-paste into public AI.  

**Knowledge Work Bench** keeps that work **on premises**: it opens a **task grant**, matches **skills** to the input, routes an **open-weight model by card**, runs retrieve / sandbox / draft under **human checkpoints**, and leaves a **Word DRAFT** soft copy with audit — while the **Gateway refuses public models** and **Monitor A** records evidence (**not** a CERT badge). Judgement stays with the engineer; the app never writes plant control systems.

Same boxes from a mid-range demo laptop to an org GPU server — scale cards and connectors, do not rewrite.

## Spoken spine (pitch, not PDF paragraphs)

1. **Hook:** Confidential knowledge work cannot leave the fence.  
2. **Product:** A workbench — grants, skills, models, drafts — not a chatbot skin.  
3. **Loop:** Attach scan → confirm extract → cite SOP or abstain → Word DRAFT → leave pack.  
4. **Second loop:** Coding card → sandbox → human accept.  
5. **Proof:** Dual tags when staged · deny `gpt-4o` · Monitor evidence ≠ CERT.  
6. **Close:** Same architecture at org scale; Approver stays outside.
