# Machinery Glossary

## Separation units
- **CDU** — Crude Distillation Unit: Separates crude oil into fractions (LPG, naphtha, kerosene, diesel, gas oil, residue) by boiling point
- **VDU** — Vacuum Distillation Unit: Separates heavy residue under vacuum
- **NSU** — Naphtha Stabilisation Unit: Removes light gases from naphtha

## Treatment units
- **NHT** — Naphtha Hydrotreater: Removes sulphur and nitrogen from naphtha
- **DHDT** — Diesel Hydrotreater: Removes sulphur from diesel to meet BS-VI
- **GOHDS** — Gas Oil Hydrodesulphurisation: Treats gas oil
- **Amine Treating** — Removes acidic gases (H₂S, CO₂)
- **Sour Water Stripper** — Removes H₂S and ammonia from wastewater
- **Sulphur Recovery Unit** — Converts H₂S to elemental sulphur

## Conversion units
- **FCC** — Fluid Catalytic Cracking: Heavy gas oil to lighter products
- **PFCC** — Petrochemical FCC: Maximises propylene
- **HCU** — Hydrocracker Unit: High-pressure hydrogen conversion
- **DCU** — Delayed Coking Unit: Thermal cracking of heavy residue
- **CCR** — Continuous Catalytic Reformer: Low-octane naphtha to high-octane reformate
- **Isomerisation** — Rearranges molecules to increase octane

## Product units
- **VBU** — Visbreaker/Bitumen Unit
- **PPU** — Polypropylene Unit
- **Aromatics/OMPL** — Para-xylene and benzene production
- **Hydrogen Generation/Recovery** — Hydrogen production and recovery

## Utilities
- Captive power plant, steam system, cooling water, desalination, effluent treatment, instrument air, nitrogen, flare, firewater, tank farm, laboratory

## Cross-sector machinery
- **Power**: Boilers, turbines, generators, transformers, switchyards, protection relays, SCADA/EMS, HVDC
- **Defence**: Aircraft assembly jigs, engine test cells, radar assembly, ballistic ranges, shipyards, ammunition lines
- **Heavy industry**: Blast furnaces, coke ovens, rolling mills, smelters, kilns, CNC machines
- **Railways**: Locomotives, coaches, signalling, tracks
- **Space**: Clean rooms, vibration test facilities, launch vehicle stages, ground stations
