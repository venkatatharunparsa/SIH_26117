# Glossary Disambiguation

Some abbreviations have multiple meanings depending on context. This file disambiguates them.

## RBI

| Context | Meaning | Related files |
|---|---|---|
| Inspection, integrity, asset management | **Risk-Based Inspection** — an API 580/581 methodology for prioritising inspection based on risk | `03-personas/02-inspection-engineer.md`, `04-workflows/02-inspection-workflows.md`, `06-document-templates/01-inspection-recommendation-note.md` |
| Banking, finance, policy | **Reserve Bank of India** — the central bank and financial regulator | `07-cross-sector/09-banking-finance.md`, `08-policy/07-rbi-guidelines.md` |

The two meanings never appear in the same context. In the workbench, RBI as Risk-Based Inspection is an inspection module; RBI as Reserve Bank of India is a regulatory policy reference.

## HAL

| Context | Meaning | Related files |
|---|---|---|
| Defence, aerospace | **Hindustan Aeronautics Limited** — a defence PSU that makes aircraft, helicopters and aero-engines | `07-cross-sector/02-defence-psus.md`, `11-reference/04-organization-coverage.md` |
| Pharmaceuticals | **Hindustan Antibiotics Limited** — a public-sector pharmaceutical company | `07-cross-sector/15-pharmaceuticals.md` |

When writing about HAL in a defence context, always write "Hindustan Aeronautics Limited (HAL)". When writing about pharma, write "Hindustan Antibiotics Limited (HAL Pharma)" or just "Hindustan Antibiotics".

## PSU

| Context | Meaning | Related files |
|---|---|---|
| All | **Public Sector Undertaking** — a government-owned corporation | Throughout |

Also seen: **CPSE** — Central Public Sector Enterprise (a subset of PSU). **DPSU** — Defence Public Sector Undertaking (a subset of PSU in the defence sector).

## DCS

DCS is used consistently for **Distributed Control System**. Not to be confused with DCU (Delayed Coking Unit).

## EAM vs CMMS

**EAM** (Enterprise Asset Management) and **CMMS** (Computerised Maintenance Management System) are used interchangeably in this knowledge base. EAM is the broader term; CMMS is the maintenance-focused subset. Both refer to the same class of system.

## MOC

MOC is used consistently for **Management of Change**. Not to be confused with MoC (Ministry of Coal) or any other government abbreviation.

## DMS

DMS is used consistently for **Document Management System**. Not to be confused with DMS (Digital Management Services) or other contexts.

## OT vs IT

**OT** (Operational Technology) refers to industrial control systems — DCS, PLC, SIS, SCADA, historians. **IT** (Information Technology) refers to enterprise systems — ERP, EAM, email, DMS. The two must remain segmented for security.

## P&ID vs PFD

**P&ID** (Piping and Instrumentation Diagram) shows every pipe, valve, instrument and equipment item in a process system. **PFD** (Process Flow Diagram) is a simpler diagram showing main process flows. Both are engineering documents but different levels of detail.

## CDU vs VDU vs DCU

**CDU** = Crude Distillation Unit. **VDU** = Vacuum Distillation Unit. **DCU** = Delayed Coking Unit. All three are distinct process units.

## ETAI

**ETAI** = Evaluating Trustworthy Artificial Intelligence — a DRDO and armed-forces framework for AI evaluation in defence contexts. Not to be confused with other frameworks.

## DAP

**DAP** = Defence Acquisition Procedure — the Indian Ministry of Defence procurement manual. Version 2020 is the current reference.

## SMLDI

**SMLDI** = Security Manual for Licensed Defence Industries — the classification and handling guidance for defence PSUs and licensed private defence manufacturers.

## CERT-In vs NCIIPC

**CERT-In** = Indian Computer Emergency Response Team — handles cybersecurity incidents across sectors. **NCIIPC** = National Critical Information Infrastructure Protection Centre — handles critical infrastructure protection. Both are relevant to the workbench but different scopes.

## Reference note

If a document uses an abbreviation that could be ambiguous, spell out the full form on first use. In the workbench UI, hovering over an abbreviation should show the full form and its context.
