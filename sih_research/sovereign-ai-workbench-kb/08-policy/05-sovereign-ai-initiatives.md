# Sovereign AI Initiatives in India

## Bhashini

Bhashini is a government language-AI initiative supporting speech, translation and multilingual communication.

Potential industrial value:

- Multilingual shift handovers
- Voice-to-text for field operators
- Translation of government and vendor documents
- Regional-language citizen services
- Speech interfaces for district administration

However, an organisation should verify whether the specific deployment can operate within the required security boundary before sending confidential content to any hosted service.

## AI4Bharat

AI4Bharat at IIT Madras works on Indian-language datasets, translation, speech and open-source models.

Potential role:

- Local-language OCR
- Technical translation
- Hindi and regional-language document search
- Voice interfaces

## BharatGen

BharatGen is a government-supported multimodal, multilingual foundation-model initiative led by IIT Bombay under DST's NM-ICPS.

Strategically relevant because it shows India is investing in models designed for Indian languages and sovereign public-service use. It should not be assumed that BharatGen is automatically suitable for classified or industrial deployment without security, licence, model-card and offline-operation review.

## AIKosh and IndiaAI compute

IndiaAI's compute and dataset pillars may provide a national route for model development and experimentation.

For high-sensitivity industrial deployment, the likely architecture would still be:

- Model development or pretraining on approved national infrastructure
- Domain adaptation in a controlled environment
- Inference and retrieval inside the enterprise enclave
- No external prompt or document flow

## Sarvam and Krutrim

Sarvam and Krutrim are Indian AI companies contributing to the domestic model ecosystem. Public claims about "sovereign" models should be distinguished from a customer-specific air-gapped deployment. A model can be Indian-developed and still require external connectivity, external support or hosted inference unless deployed locally.

## Public-sector AI tenders and deployments

Public evidence shows increasing adoption of:

- Predictive maintenance
- Digital logbooks
- Asset-management analytics
- RPA
- Customer-service AI
- Fraud detection
- Translation and speech
- Cybersecurity analytics

The public record is much thinner on complete air-gapped LLM deployments inside Indian PSUs. That is unsurprising because details of sensitive architecture, model placement and data flows are often not disclosed.

## Credible go-to-market claim

> India has public sovereign-AI initiatives and widespread enterprise AI adoption, but there remains a gap between a general-purpose model and a deployable, permission-aware, air-gapped workbench for sensitive organisational documents.
