# Defence Security Manual

## Classification levels

The Ministry of Defence Security Manual for Licensed Defence Industries (revised June 2025) defines five levels:

| Level | Definition |
|---|---|
| Top Secret | Unauthorised disclosure could cause exceptionally grave damage to national security or national interest |
| Secret | Unauthorised disclosure could cause serious damage to national security or national interest |
| Confidential | Unauthorised disclosure could cause damage to national security, national interests or government functioning |
| Restricted | Official-use information |
| Unclassified | Requires no security classification |

## SMLDI requirements

The SMLDI applies to licensed defence industries and requires protection of government-classified information, classification policies and secure handling.

Practical implications include:

- Need-to-know access
- Cleared personnel
- Controlled document movement
- Physical security
- Secure storage
- Visitor and contractor controls
- Restrictions on copying and photography
- Secure communications
- Classification markings
- Document accountability
- Incident reporting
- Separation between classified and non-classified networks

## DAP 2020

DAP 2020 includes restricted information under the Official Secrets framework and controls technical/commercial proposals and protected acquisition information.

## Why external cloud LLM is unacceptable for classified work

- Classified data could cross the security boundary
- Model vendors may operate under foreign jurisdiction
- Prompts and logs may be retained
- Retrieval indexes may replicate sensitive content
- A model could combine individually unclassified fragments into sensitive conclusions
- Remote support or model updates may introduce an uncontrolled path
- Training data provenance may be unclear

## AI use cases for defence

A defence AI workbench could support:

- Search across approved design records
- Configuration and revision comparison
- Test-report summarisation
- Non-conformance analysis
- Drawing and specification cross-reference
- Procurement compliance
- Supplier-document review
- Maintenance-manual retrieval
- Lessons-learned search
- Secure translation and transcription
- Requirements traceability

It must not autonomously approve:

- Weapon designs
- Flight safety decisions
- Test release
- Classified dissemination
- Targeting or mission decisions
- Safety-critical manufacturing changes
