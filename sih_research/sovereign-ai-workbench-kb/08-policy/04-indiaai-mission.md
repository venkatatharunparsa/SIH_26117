# IndiaAI Mission

The IndiaAI Mission was approved with a reported outlay of approximately ₹10,371.92 crore and seven pillars.

## Seven pillars

1. Compute capacity (38,000 GPUs planned)
2. Foundation models
3. Datasets
4. Application development
5. Skills
6. Responsible AI
7. Startup financing

## AIKosh

AIKosh is positioned as a national platform for datasets, models, toolkits and AI development resources.

## BharatGen

BharatGen was launched under the Department of Science and Technology's National Mission on Interdisciplinary Cyber-Physical Systems and is described as a government-supported multimodal, multilingual foundation-model initiative.

## What these initiatives enable

- Access to foundational models for Indian languages
- Shared datasets for AI development
- GPU compute for model training
- Model testing and evaluation frameworks
- Standards for responsible AI

## What these initiatives do NOT solve

A refinery or defence PSU still needs:

- Local document retrieval
- Security integration
- Permission enforcement
- OT/IT segmentation
- Audit trails
- Data classification
- Domain evaluation
- Offline update processes
- Approved model and hardware supply chains

## Product positioning

The workbench should be positioned as **complementary** to IndiaAI Mission infrastructure:

- Model development or pretraining on approved national infrastructure
- Domain adaptation in a controlled environment
- Inference and retrieval inside the enterprise enclave
- No external prompt or document flow
- Compatible with models from AIKosh/BharatGen ecosystem
