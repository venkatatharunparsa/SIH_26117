# Indian AI Ecosystem

| Initiative | Relevant capability | Gap for your product |
|---|---|---|
| Bhashini | Indian-language translation and speech | Not a complete confidential industrial knowledge platform |
| AI4Bharat | Indic language datasets/models | Requires industrial connectors, governance and deployment |
| BharatGen | Government-supported multilingual/multimodal model initiative | Domain integration, classification and air-gapped customer deployment remain separate |
| AIKosh/IndiaAI | Datasets, models, compute and AI ecosystem | Does not automatically provide enterprise document-control workflows |
| Sarvam | Indian-language models and AI products | Need customer-specific local deployment, security and workflow integration |
| Krutrim | Indian model ecosystem | Same qualification issue: model origin is not equal to air-gapped operation |

BharatGen is a government-supported multimodal foundation-model initiative under DST and IIT Bombay. AIKosh is positioned as a national platform for datasets, models and AI development.

## Key distinction

A model can be Indian-developed and still require external connectivity, external support or hosted inference unless deployed locally.

## Product positioning

The workbench should be positioned as complementary to Indian sovereign AI:

- Use Indian-developed models where appropriate
- Deploy locally or on-premise
- Add domain-specific connectors and workflows
- Add permission-aware retrieval and audit lineage
- Preserve sovereign operation end-to-end
