# What Is Missing

## The gap

The market is crowded with:

- Models
- RAG frameworks
- Vector databases
- Local chat interfaces
- Cloud infrastructure
- Governance dashboards

The missing layer is a **deployable, security-aware operational knowledge system** that understands:

- Assets
- Tags
- Revisions
- Approvals
- Work orders
- Contracts
- Incidents
- Standards
- Roles
- Document lineage
- Real deliverables

## The "why us" case

The differentiation is integration, not invention:

1. **Industrial entity resolution** — Link equipment tags, work orders, historian tags, drawings, inspection points and vendor reports
2. **Revision-aware retrieval** — Return the latest approved document and identify superseded versions
3. **Permission-preserving search** — Enforce existing access rights at document, section, page or record level
4. **Classification-aware model routing** — Use small local models for routine extraction, larger models for complex reasoning and restricted models for sensitive enclaves
5. **Agentic workflows** — Execute controlled sequences such as MOC review, RCA preparation or TBE generation
6. **Real deliverables** — Produce Word, Excel and PowerPoint outputs using customer templates
7. **Evidence lineage** — Link every number, chart and conclusion to a source
8. **Air-gap proof** — Document network paths, offline operation, model supply chain, update procedure and telemetry controls
9. **Cross-sector ontology** — Support equipment, projects, tenders, cases, policies, accounts, customers and documents
10. **Approval gates** — Drafting is automated; safety, legal, financial and statutory decisions remain human-controlled

## The defensible market claim

> Indian refineries, power utilities, defence manufacturers, government departments, regulators, financial institutions, heavy industries and legal bodies all perform high-volume knowledge work over documents and operational data that is often too sensitive for consumer cloud AI. A secure local workbench can reduce search, extraction, reconciliation and document-production effort while preserving access control, auditability and human approval.

That is a stronger and more accurate thesis than claiming every organisation needs the same physical deployment.
