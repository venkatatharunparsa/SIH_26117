# What Is Not Confidential

Public availability does not mean every copy or context is safe. A published annual report is public; an internal draft of the next annual report is not.

## By sector

| Sector | Usually public/non-sensitive |
|---|---|
| Refineries and PSUs | Annual reports, audited financial statements, stock-exchange filings, published tenders, press releases, recruitment ads, CSR reports, sustainability reports, published environmental clearances, investor presentations, public product brochures |
| Defence | Published policies, recruitment, general capability statements, public annual reports, defence-procurement manuals, published tender notices, DRDO technology-foresight documents |
| Government | Gazette notifications, enacted laws, published schemes, approved budgets, press releases, parliamentary answers, lawfully-released RTI disclosures, public service rules |
| Banking | Published financial statements, product terms, published interest rates, RBI circulars, public investor disclosures, public branch/service information |
| Legal | Published judgments, public court orders, enacted laws, gazette notifications, published rules, public filings |
| Heavy industry | Annual reports, public sustainability reports, product brochures, published environmental statements, published tenders, recruitment material |
| Railways | Published timetables, fare tables, annual reports, statistics, public tender notices, safety advisories released for public use |
| Regulators | Public regulations, released consultation papers, final orders, annual reports, public standards and guidance |
| Space | Public mission summaries, post-release launch announcements, annual reports, public technology descriptions |
| Atomic energy | Public annual reports, safety codes and guides released publicly, public recruitment, non-sensitive educational material |
| Telecom | Published tariffs, licence conditions, annual reports, public service info, public consultation papers |
| Pharma | Approved product labels, public regulatory guidance, published scientific papers, public annual reports |
| Ports | Published vessel notices, tariffs, annual reports, public tenders, published port statistics |
| Mining | Annual reports, published production statistics, public environmental clearances, public tenders, investor presentations |
| Fertilizers | Annual reports, product brochures, public tenders, published prices/subsidy info, public environmental approvals |
| Food/agriculture | Published procurement rules, public schemes, market notifications, public statistics, annual reports |
| Textiles | Product brochures, annual reports, public tenders, published schemes, recruitment ads |

## Critical rule

Even a public document may contain non-public elements:

- A published annual report is public, but the internal draft is not
- A published tender notice is public, but bidder evaluations and negotiation notes are not
- A published environmental clearance is public, but the underlying monitoring data may not be
- A published judgment is public, but sealed exhibits and case diaries are not

The AI workbench must classify at the **paragraph level**, not just the document level.
