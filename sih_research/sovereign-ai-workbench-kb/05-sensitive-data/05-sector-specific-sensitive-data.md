# Sector-Specific Sensitive Data

## Oil and gas refineries
Process data, P&IDs, OT architecture, inspection thickness data, corrosion rates, crude economics, vendor bids, incident reports, turnaround schedules, safety-critical operating limits, board notes.

## Power generation and transmission
Grid topology, SCADA/EMS configuration, protection relay settings, outage plans, unit availability, fuel inventory, PPA economics, cybersecurity vulnerabilities, generator performance, transformer condition, dam and emergency plans.

## Defence PSUs
Classified drawings, test data, military requirements, configuration baselines, supplier qualification files, classified correspondence, export-control records, technical proposals, acceptance documents.

## DRDO laboratories
Military requirements, prototype designs, test-range data, sensor signatures, propulsion and guidance data, vulnerability assessments, source code, unpublished research, collaborator and vendor data.

## Government ministries
Cabinet proposals, personal and welfare data, land ownership and acquisition, law-and-order info, investigation files, legal privilege, unreleased policy and budget, security and emergency plans.

## State and district administration
Land records, beneficiary data, disaster management files, law-and-order reports, court and quasi-judicial files, welfare scheme data, personal data, procurement records.

## Regulators (AERB, CEA, PNGRB, CPCB, OISD, RBI, SEBI, IRDAI, TRAI)
Licensee submissions, safety analyses, incident data, non-compliance records, enforcement strategy, market-sensitive information, supervisory returns, payment data, operator submissions, tariff data.

## Heavy manufacturing
Process recipes, heat/charge sheets, quality certificates, refractory reports, NDT and weld records, energy intensity, equipment failure patterns, supplier audits, vendor contracts, environmental returns.

## Railways
Signalling and interlocking configuration, control-room info, infrastructure weaknesses, maintenance deferments, accident investigations, passenger personal and payment data, security arrangements, pre-release train schedules.

## Banking
KYC data, account data, credit scores and models, suspicious-transaction reports, fraud investigations, supervisory information, investment strategy, cyber incidents.

## Insurance
Customer identity and account data, medical records, claims evidence, actuarial models, fraud investigations, investment strategy, regulatory correspondence.

## Legal and judicial
Pleadings, affidavits, sealed records, legal opinions, investigation files, case diaries, forensic records, privileged correspondence, witness statements.

## Space
Satellite designs, launch-vehicle technology, mission procedures, ground-station details, telemetry, payload performance, unreleased launch schedules, dual-use technology.

## Atomic energy
Reactor design, safety-system configuration, security arrangements, vulnerability assessments, radiation events, fuel-cycle information, emergency plans.

## Telecom
Network architecture, lawful-interception information, subscriber data, billing records, cyber incident reports, vendor contracts, capacity plans.

## Pharmaceuticals
Clinical and patient data, formulation and process recipes, stability and trial results, patent drafts, manufacturing deviations, regulatory strategy, supplier data.

## Ports and shipping
Cargo type and quantity, vessel movements, dangerous goods, port security arrangements, customs investigations, critical equipment outages, customer and shipping contracts.

## Mining
Reserves, grade and geological models, mine plans, production costs, safety vulnerabilities, environmental non-compliance, dispatch and stockpile locations.

## Fertilizers
Process recipes, ammonia and hazardous-inventory information, energy intensity, plant constraints, vendor terms, distribution strategy.

## Food and agriculture
Procurement prices and bids, strategic stock locations, distribution plans, farmer and beneficiary data, research and breeding data, warehouse vulnerabilities.

## Textiles
Colour and process recipes, design IP, customer order information, production costs, vendor terms, market strategy.

## Private defence manufacturers
RFP and statement of work, system requirement specifications, compliance matrices, technical proposals, CAD drawings, manufacturing process sheets, test plans and reports, non-conformance reports, configuration baselines, quality dossiers, offset plans, export-control records.

## Cross-sector patterns

| Common sensitive category | Universal examples |
|---|---|
| Cyber/OT | Network architecture, protection settings, control-system config |
| Personal | Identity, health, payroll, contractor records |
| Commercial | Vendor bids, negotiations, contracts, pricing |
| Safety | Incident reports, HAZOP, safety-critical equipment |
| Legal | Legal opinions, litigation, privilege |
| Strategic | Unreleased plans, budgets, board notes |
| IP | Patents, process recipes, source code, designs |
| Security | Physical security, emergency plans, access controls |
