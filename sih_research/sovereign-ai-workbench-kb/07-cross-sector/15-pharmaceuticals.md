# Pharmaceuticals

## Organisation types

CDSCO and regulators, NIPERs and research institutes, public-sector pharmaceutical companies (HAL, IDPL, KAPL, Bengal Chemicals, Hindustan Antibiotics), private pharmaceutical manufacturers, ICMR laboratories, biotechnology and vaccine institutes.

## Processes

Discovery and research, formulation development, analytical method development, clinical trials, manufacturing, batch release, QA/QC, regulatory submissions, pharmacovigilance, supply chain.

## Documents

Laboratory notebooks, batch manufacturing records, SOPs, deviation and CAPA reports, stability data, clinical-trial reports, regulatory submissions, validation protocols, certificates of analysis, patents, vendor qualification.

## Sensitive information

Clinical and patient data, formulation and process recipes, stability and trial results, patent drafts, manufacturing deviations, regulatory strategy, supplier data.

## AI uses

- Regulatory dossier search
- Deviation/CAPA analysis
- Batch-record review
- Literature and patent search
- Clinical-document summarisation
- SOP comparison
- Pharmacovigilance case review

## Constraints

- Clinical data protection (DPDP, CDSCO)
- Patient privacy
- IP protection for formulations
- Regulatory submission confidentiality
- GMP compliance
