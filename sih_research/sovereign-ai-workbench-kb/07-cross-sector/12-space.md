# Space

## Organisation map

- ISRO
- Department of Space
- NSIL
- IN-SPACe
- Antrix legacy/commercial functions
- Centres: VSSC, URSC, SAC, SDSC, LPSC, ISTRAC, NRSC, IISU, Master Control Facilities
- Indian Institute of Space Science and Technology

## Processes and machinery

Satellite systems engineering, payload development, propulsion, launch-vehicle stages, avionics, clean-room integration, environmental and vibration testing, launch operations, ground stations, mission control, telemetry, tracking and command, earth-observation data processing.

## Documents

Mission requirements, system design, interface-control documents, test reports, failure reviews, launch procedures, flight-readiness review, telemetry and anomaly logs, ground-station procedures, technology-transfer documents.

## Sensitive information

Satellite designs, launch-vehicle technology, mission procedures, ground-station details, telemetry, payload performance, unreleased launch schedules, strategic or dual-use technology.

## AI uses

- Requirements traceability
- Design-review assistance
- Test-result analysis
- Telemetry anomaly detection
- Mission-procedure search
- Failure-review chronology
- Technical-report generation

## Deployment posture

On-premise or isolated infrastructure. ISRO publishes annual reports and mission achievements, but detailed design and mission-control information is not public.
