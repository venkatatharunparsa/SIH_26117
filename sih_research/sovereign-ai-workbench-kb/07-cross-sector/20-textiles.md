# Textiles

## Processes

Fibre procurement, spinning, weaving, dyeing and finishing, garment manufacture, quality control, design and merchandising, marketing.

## Documents

Production records, yarn and fabric specifications, colour recipes, quality reports, design files, vendor contracts, customer specifications, maintenance and utility records.

## Sensitive information

Colour and process recipes, design IP, customer order information, production costs, vendor terms, market strategy.

## AI uses

- Defect analysis
- Production planning
- Recipe search
- Vendor comparison
- Design-document retrieval
- Energy and water optimisation

## Sample organisations

NTC, handloom corporations, jute corporations, Cotton Corporation of India, Jute Corporation of India, National Handloom Development Corporation, state textile mills and handloom boards, textile research associations.
