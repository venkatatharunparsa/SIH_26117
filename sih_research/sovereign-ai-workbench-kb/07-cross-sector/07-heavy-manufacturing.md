# Heavy Manufacturing

## Process archetypes

| Sector | Main process chain |
|---|---|
| Steel | Raw materials → coke/sinter/pellets → blast furnace/DRI → steelmaking → casting → rolling/finishing |
| Aluminium | Bauxite mining → alumina refining → smelting → casting/rolling |
| Cement | Quarry → crushing → raw mill → kiln → clinker → cement mill → dispatch |
| Shipbuilding | Design → material procurement → fabrication → block assembly → hull integration → testing/sea trials |
| Engineering/manufacturing | Design → procurement → machining/fabrication → assembly → testing → delivery |

## Machinery

Blast furnaces, coke ovens, sinter plants, basic-oxygen or electric furnaces, continuous casters, rolling mills, kilns and mills, smelter potlines, anode plants, CNC machines, welding and NDT equipment, cranes and material-handling systems, MES, QMS, ERP, EAM and historians.

## Departments

Production, process/metallurgy, quality/laboratory, maintenance/reliability, inspection, projects, supply chain, HSE/environment, energy, sales and customer quality, finance, HR, IT/OT, legal.

## Documents

Process recipes, heat/charge sheets, quality certificates, lab results, maintenance work orders, equipment history, energy reports, refractory reports, NDT and weld records, supplier audits, vendor contracts, environmental returns, customer complaints, capital-project files.

## Sensitive data

Production recipes, heat/charge parameters, quality and yield data, energy intensity, equipment failure patterns, plant bottlenecks, procurement and pricing, expansion plans, refractory life, supplier audits.

- Refractory-life analysis
