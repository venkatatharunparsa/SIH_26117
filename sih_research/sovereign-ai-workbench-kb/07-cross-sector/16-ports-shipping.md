# Ports and Shipping

## Processes

Vessel arrival and berth planning, pilotage and marine operations, cargo handling, yard and warehouse management, customs and documentation, security, equipment maintenance, environmental compliance, billing and customer service.

## Documents

Vessel schedules, berthing plans, cargo manifests, bills of lading, customs documents, dangerous-goods declarations, crane and equipment records, security plans, incident reports, vendor contracts, port-state correspondence.

## Sensitive information

Cargo type and quantity, vessel movements, dangerous goods, port security arrangements, customs investigations, critical equipment outages, customer and shipping contracts.

## AI uses

- Berth scheduling
- Cargo-document extraction
- Customs-document checking
- Equipment maintenance
- Dangerous-goods compliance
- Incident chronology

## Sample organisations

JNPT/Nhava Sheva, Mumbai Port, New Mangalore Port, Cochin Port, Chennai Port, Kolkata/Haldia, Deendayal/Kandla, Mormugao, Visakhapatnam, Paradip, V.O. Chidambaranar/Tuticorin, Syama Prasad Mookerjee Port, Kamarajar, inland waterways bodies, Cochin Shipyard.
