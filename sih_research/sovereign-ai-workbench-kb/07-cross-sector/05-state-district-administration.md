# State and District Administration

## Functions

- Land records and revenue administration
- Mutation and registration
- Disaster-management files
- Welfare and beneficiary data
- Caste, income and residence certificates
- Police and magistrate correspondence
- Court and quasi-judicial files
- Procurement and works records
- Health and education programme data
- Local-body budgets

## Systems

State e-Governance systems, land records, treasury, HRMS, district portals, beneficiary management systems, RTI portals, court-case systems, paper files and scanned legacy records.

## Documents

Government orders, land records, beneficiary data, disaster reports, scheme implementation reports, court and quasi-judicial files, procurement records, health and education programme data, local-body budgets.

## Sensitive information

Land ownership and disputes, personal data, law-and-order reports, disaster-management plans, beneficiary data, court files, procurement records.

## AI uses

- File search
- Scheme monitoring
- Land-record queries
- Disaster response
- Beneficiary verification
- Court-case chronology
- Audit response preparation
- Multilingual citizen communication

## Constraints

- Personal data protection (DPDP Act)
- Judicial confidentiality for quasi-judicial files
- Law-and-order sensitivity
- Coordination with state and central systems
- Language diversity
