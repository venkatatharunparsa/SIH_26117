# Government Ministries

## Operating structure

| Layer | Typical work |
|---|---|
| Union ministry | Policy, legislation, schemes, regulation, budget, Parliament and Cabinet |
| Department/attached office | Programme administration, technical review, procurement and implementation |
| State secretariat | State policy, rules, budget, cabinet matters, interdepartmental coordination |
| District administration | Revenue, land, welfare, disaster response, law and order, local coordination |
| Block/local administration | Beneficiary delivery, records, inspections, certificates, local works |

## Systems

e-Office/e-file, NIC email, PFMS, Treasury and IFMS, HRMS, GeM and e-procurement, land-record systems, beneficiary and scheme portals, RTI portal, court/legal case systems, GIS and disaster-management systems, paper files and scanned archives.

## Daily workflow: government approval file

1. Receipt section registers a letter or electronic reference
2. Dealing assistant opens an e-file
3. Previous related files are searched
4. Facts and rules are extracted
5. A note is drafted
6. Supporting documents are attached
7. Section officer checks facts and formatting
8. Under Secretary/Deputy Secretary reviews
9. Finance, law or technical departments are consulted
10. Secretary or minister's office receives the file
11. Decision is recorded
12. Government order or communication is drafted
13. Dispatch and follow-up are logged
14. The file remains available for audit, Parliament, RTI or court review

## Documents

File noting, draft government order, cabinet note, interdepartmental consultation, budget sanction, scheme guidelines, parliamentary answer, RTI reply, audit response, legal opinion, tender and contract, inspection report, beneficiary list, district implementation report.

## Sensitive information

Cabinet proposals before decision, personal and welfare data, land ownership and acquisition, law-and-order information, investigation files, legal privilege, unreleased policy and budget, security and emergency plans.

## AI uses

- File chronology
- Note summarisation
- Rule and precedent search
- Drafting of replies
- Policy comparison
- RTI-exemption flagging
- Audit-response preparation
- Multilingual translation
- Scheme-progress extraction

The AI should never decide whether information is legally exempt from RTI without an authorised officer or legal review.
