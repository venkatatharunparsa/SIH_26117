# Legal and Judicial

## Workflows

- Case filing and registration
- Pleading and affidavit preparation
- Evidence indexing
- Hearing preparation
- Legal-research memorandum
- Order and judgment drafting
- Government legal opinion
- Investigation and prosecution
- Appeal and compliance tracking

## Documents

Plaint and written statement, petition and affidavit, exhibits, witness statements, court orders, judgments, legal opinions, investigation files, case diaries, forensic records, privileged correspondence, settlement and arbitration documents.

## Sensitive information

Privileged advice, sealed records, personal data, investigation files, national-security matters, witness protection information, chain-of-custody data.

## AI uses

- Case-law retrieval
- Citation checking
- Pleading comparison
- Chronology extraction
- Exhibit indexing
- Redaction
- Translation
- Hearing-bundle creation

## Constraints

- Legal privilege
- Sealed records
- Judicial independence
- Evidence integrity
- Personal data
- Chain of custody
- Human accountability for legal conclusions

## Sample organisations

Supreme Court, High Courts, district courts, government legal cells, law departments, CBI, ED, NIA, state police, Central Administrative Tribunal, NCLT/NCLAT, consumer courts, family courts, special courts, prosecution directorates, Legal Services Authorities, forensic laboratories.
