# Telecom

## Organisations

BSNL, MTNL, C-DOT, TCIL, RailTel, ITI Limited, Department of Telecommunications, partner ecosystem, telecom service providers, critical telecom vendors.

## Operations

- Core network
- Radio/access network
- Fibre and transmission
- OSS/BSS
- Billing
- Customer care
- Numbering and provisioning
- Lawful-interception compliance
- Cybersecurity and incident response

## Documents and data

Network diagrams, configuration records, change requests, outage reports, billing records, subscriber data, cyber incident reports, vendor contracts, capacity plans, regulatory submissions.

## Sensitive information

Network architecture, lawful-interception information, subscriber data, billing records, cyber vulnerabilities, capacity plans, critical equipment outages.

## AI uses

- Alarm triage
- Outage chronology
- Capacity planning
- Customer-service document search
- Fraud detection
- Configuration comparison
- Incident-report drafting

## Deployment posture

Network architecture, lawful-interception information, subscriber data and vulnerabilities should not be exposed to consumer AI. Segmented deployment with strict access controls is required for sensitive data.
