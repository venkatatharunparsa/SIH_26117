# Cross-Sector Overview

The problem is not unique to MRPL. The same structural barrier appears across Indian oil and gas, power, defence, government, regulation, heavy manufacturing, banking, insurance and legal work:

- Critical knowledge is distributed across ERP, EAM, historians, DCS/SCADA, LIMS, email, shared drives, portals and paper.
- The most valuable documents are often confidential, safety-critical, commercially sensitive, personally identifiable or security-classified.
- Staff need AI assistance for search, comparison, calculation, drafting and summarisation.
- Public cloud AI creates concerns about data transmission, retention, jurisdiction, model training, privileged access, cyberattack and loss of audit control.

The strongest product positioning is not that every customer legally requires a fully air-gapped deployment. Rather:

> Many customers need a deployment option in which sensitive data, prompts, retrieval indexes, model inference, logs and generated documents remain inside their controlled security boundary.

## Common workflows across sectors

1. Receive a request, event, defect, proposal or regulatory requirement
2. Search multiple systems
3. Find the latest approved documents
4. Extract structured data from PDFs, spreadsheets, images or emails
5. Reconcile conflicting values
6. Perform technical, financial or legal analysis
7. Draft a note, report, evaluation or recommendation
8. Obtain cross-functional comments
9. Route for approval
10. Store the final decision and evidence
11. Track follow-up actions
12. Produce an audit trail
