# Regulators

| Regulator | Main domain | Typical sensitive submissions |
|---|---|---|
| AERB | Nuclear and radiation safety | Safety analysis, facility design, radiation incidents |
| CEA | Electricity planning and standards | Grid studies, utility data, protection and reliability information |
| PNGRB | Petroleum/gas pipelines and CGD | Pipeline maps, capacity, licensee commercial and safety data |
| CPCB | Pollution and environmental compliance | Emissions, hazardous waste, non-compliance and enforcement |
| OISD | Petroleum safety | Incident reports, safety audits and refinery compliance |
| RBI | Banking and payment systems | Supervisory returns, inspections, payment data, enforcement |
| SEBI | Securities markets | Investigations, market-sensitive information, issuer submissions |
| IRDAI | Insurance | Insurer solvency, policyholder and claims data, supervisory reports |
| TRAI | Telecom | Operator submissions, network and tariff data, regulatory investigations |

AERB publishes annual reports, codes and guides concerning safety review and inspection. PNGRB publishes regulatory guidelines for petroleum and natural-gas infrastructure. OISD publishes incident-reporting and safety material.

## Workflow: licence/compliance review

1. Applicant submits application and annexures through portal/email
2. Registry checks completeness
3. Technical officer opens a case file
4. Applicable rules and prior decisions are retrieved
5. Drawings, safety analyses and certificates are reviewed
6. Inspection is scheduled
7. Inspectors create field notes and photographs
8. Applicant receives queries
9. Applicant submits clarification
10. Technical committee reviews
11. Legal or enforcement department comments
12. Decision/order is drafted
13. Competent authority signs
14. Compliance conditions and future inspection dates are recorded

## Sensitive information

- Licensee submissions
- Safety analyses
- Incident data
- Non-compliance records
- Enforcement strategy
- Market-sensitive information (SEBI, RBI)
- Payment data (RBI)
- Operator submissions (TRAI)
- Tariff data (PNGRB, TRAI)

## AI uses

- Completeness checks
- Clause-to-evidence mapping
- Previous-decision retrieval
- Inspection-report comparison
- Non-compliance extraction
- Query-draft generation
- Enforcement chronology
- Public summary after redaction

## Constraints

- Separation between public guidance and licensee-confidential material
- Separation between internal review and enforcement strategy
- Protection of investigations and supervisory information
- Multilingual public communication after redaction
- Coordination with courts and appellate bodies
