# Insurance

## Operations

- Proposal and underwriting
- Policy issuance
- Premium and renewals
- Claims registration
- Survey/investigation
- Medical assessment
- Reserving and actuarial analysis
- Reinsurance
- Fraud investigation
- Regulatory reporting
- Investment management

## Documents

Policy documents, proposal forms, medical reports, claim files, survey reports, investigation reports, actuarial models, reinsurance treaties, regulatory correspondence, investment records, board notes.

## Sensitive information

Policyholder identity and medical data, claims evidence, actuarial models, fraud investigations, reinsurance and investment strategy, supervisory information.

## AI uses

- Claims-document extraction
- Underwriting evidence search
- Policy comparison
- Fraud-case chronology
- Medical-record summarisation (with strict access)
- Regulatory-report drafting
- Actuarial data analysis (advisory only)
- Customer-service document search

## Constraints

- IRDAI regulatory requirements
- Personal and health data protection
- Human accountability for claim decisions
- Explainability of underwriting decisions
- Fraud investigation confidentiality

## Sample organisations

LIC, GIC, New India Assurance, United India, Oriental Insurance, National Insurance, Agriculture Insurance Company, ECGC, IRDAI (regulator).
