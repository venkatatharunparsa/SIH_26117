# Mining

## Processes

Geological exploration, mine planning, drilling and blasting, excavation and haulage, crushing and beneficiation, stockpile management, rail/road dispatch, mine safety, environmental rehabilitation.

## Machinery

Drill rigs, shovels and excavators, dumpers, crushers, conveyors, beneficiation plants, slurry pipelines, weighbridges, fleet-management systems.

## Documents

Geological models, mine plans, drilling logs, blasting plans, production reports, fleet records, safety reports, environmental monitoring, dispatch records, contractor documents.

## Sensitive information

Reserves, grade and geological models, mine plans, production costs, safety vulnerabilities, environmental non-compliance, dispatch and stockpile locations.

## AI uses

- Geological search
- Grade prediction
- Fleet and production optimisation
- Safety-event analysis
- Environmental-report drafting
- Contractor compliance

## Sample organisations

Coal India, NMDC, MOIL, Hindustan Zinc, Hindustan Copper, SCCL, NLC India, Uranium Corporation, Odisha Mining Corporation, state mineral development corporations, Geological Survey of India.

NMDC publicly reports large-scale mechanised iron-ore mining, including 45.02 million tonnes of production in FY2023–24 and revenue of ₹21,294 crore.
