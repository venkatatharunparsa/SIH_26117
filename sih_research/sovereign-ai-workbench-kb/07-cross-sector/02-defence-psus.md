## Constraints

The SMLDI requires licensed defence industries to safeguard government-classified information and implement a classification policy. DAP 2020 includes restricted information under the Official Secrets framework and controls technical/commercial proposals and protected acquisition information.

For Secret and Top Secret material, use a cleared, segmented or air-gapped environment. For unclassified production and publicly released material, a controlled private cloud may be sufficient.
