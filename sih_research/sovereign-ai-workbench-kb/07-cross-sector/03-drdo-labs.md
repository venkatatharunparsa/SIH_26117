# DRDO Laboratories

## Profile and structure

DRDO is the R&D wing of the Ministry of Defence, tasked with critical defence technologies and systems for the armed forces.

Public directories list laboratories including: ADE (Aeronautical Development Establishment), ADA (Aeronautical Development Agency), GTRE (Gas Turbine Research Establishment), DEBEL (Defence Bio-Engineering and Electro-Medical Laboratory), CAIR (Centre for Artificial Intelligence and Robotics), LRDE (Electronics and Radar Development Establishment), DARE (Defence Avionics Research Establishment), ADRDE (Aerial Delivery Research and Development Establishment), ARDE (Armament Research and Development Establishment), RCI (Research Centre Imarat), CVRDE (Combat Vehicles Research and Development Establishment), DRDL (Defence Research and Development Laboratory), ITR (Integrated Test Range), PXE (Proof and Experimental Establishment), NMRL (Naval Materials Research Laboratory), DEAL (Defence Electronics Applications Laboratory), MTRDC (Microwave Tube Research and Development Centre).

DRDO's public material identifies AI/ML technology development.

## Processes

- User-requirement interpretation
- Research proposal
- Technology-risk assessment
- Concept design
- Mathematical modelling
- Simulation
- Prototype
- Laboratory test
- Field trial
- User evaluation
- Technology transfer
- Productionisation
- Publication or classification decision

## Documents

Research proposal, project sanction, experimental design, laboratory notebook, simulation output, source code, test procedure, test-range data, failure and anomaly report, design review, technology-readiness assessment, technology-transfer package, patent filing, classified correspondence, vendor and academic collaboration agreement.

## Workforce

Scientists, research fellows, design and test engineers, technical officers, instrumentation specialists, programme managers, security and document-control staff, industry and academic collaborators.

## AI uses

- Research-paper and internal-report search
- Experiment-log summarisation
- Test-result comparison
- Simulation-result indexing
- Configuration and version control
- Patent and prior-art search
- Technology-transfer package generation
- Secure code and model review

## Sensitive data

Military requirements, prototype designs, test-range data, sensor signatures, propulsion and guidance data, vulnerability assessments, source code, unpublished research, collaborator and vendor data.

## Trust controls

- Model and dataset approval
- No external inference
- Cleared-user access
- Separate repositories by classification
- Complete retrieval and export logs
- Human review of scientific conclusions
- No autonomous design approval

DRDO and the armed forces launched the ETAI framework for trustworthy AI in 2024, underscoring the need for reliability, safety, security, transparency and evaluation in defence AI.
