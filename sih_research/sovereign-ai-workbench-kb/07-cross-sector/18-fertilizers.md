# Fertilizers

## Processes

Natural-gas or feedstock handling, ammonia production, urea synthesis and granulation, phosphoric acid and complex fertiliser production, utilities and storage, product quality and distribution.

## Machinery

Reformers, ammonia converters, compressors, high-pressure synthesis loops, granulators, evaporators, storage tanks, bagging and dispatch systems.

## Documents

Process design basis, catalyst records, operating reports, quality certificates, safety studies, emissions and effluent reports, turnaround work packs, distribution records, subsidy and commercial contracts.

## Sensitive information

Process recipes, ammonia and hazardous-inventory information, energy intensity, plant constraints, vendor terms, distribution strategy.

## AI uses

- Energy optimisation
- Process troubleshooting
- Safety-procedure search
- Quality prediction
- Turnaround planning
- Compliance reporting

## Sample organisations

FACT, RCF, GSFC, NFL, Chambal Fertilisers, IFFCO, KRIBHCO, Coromandel, Paradeep Phosphates, Madras Fertilizers, Fertilizer Corporation legacy sites, Department of Fertilizers.
