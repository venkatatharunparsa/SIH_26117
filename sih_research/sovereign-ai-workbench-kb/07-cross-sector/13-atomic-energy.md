NPCIL is a Department of Atomic Energy PSU and publishes annual reports, but reactor-security details are not public.

## Processes

- Reactor design and operation
- Fuel fabrication and handling
- Radiation protection
- Reactor maintenance
- Waste management
- Emergency preparedness
- Nuclear materials research
- Regulatory review

## Documents

Safety analysis reports, technical specifications, operating procedures, surveillance records, radiation-monitoring results, event reports, maintenance and work permits, emergency plans, training and qualification records, regulatory submissions.

## Sensitive information

Reactor design, safety-system configuration, security arrangements, vulnerability assessments, radiation events, fuel-cycle information, emergency plans.

## AI uses

- Procedure search
- Safety-case evidence mapping
- Training assistants
- Event and surveillance trend analysis
- Regulatory compliance mapping

## Deployment posture

For nuclear-security data, on-premise segregated or air-gapped deployment is the prudent default. AERB publicly describes its safety review and regulatory role, but security-sensitive plant information is not public.
