## Security constraints

The SMLDI applies to licensed defence industries and requires protection of government-classified information, classification policies and secure handling.

Private defence manufacturers also face:

- DAP 2020 requirements
- Official Secrets restrictions
- Customer security clauses
- Export-control obligations
- Industrial security audits
- Facility and personnel clearance
- Supplier confidentiality
- IP and technology-transfer restrictions
- Contract-specific data-residency requirements

## Offset obligations

Defence offsets depend on applicable acquisition category, RFP terms, contract value and threshold, offset policy in force, eligible products/services, discharge and verification requirements, and government approval and reporting.

A private company may have obligations relating to local sourcing, technology transfer, investment in Indian defence capability, export or production commitments, partner and supplier development, and offset-credit documentation.

Offset files should be treated as commercially and strategically sensitive, with contract-level permissions.
