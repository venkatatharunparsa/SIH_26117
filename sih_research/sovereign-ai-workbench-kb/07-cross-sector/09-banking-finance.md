# Banking and Finance

## Banking operations

1. Customer onboarding and KYC
2. Account opening and servicing
3. Credit origination
4. Credit appraisal and sanction
5. Disbursement and monitoring
6. Transaction processing
7. AML/fraud monitoring
8. Recovery and legal action
9. Audit and regulatory reporting
10. Treasury and risk management

## Systems

Core banking, CRM, loan-origination system, document-management system, AML and transaction-monitoring system, fraud platform, credit bureau, treasury, HRMS, regulatory reporting, email and case-management systems.

## Documents

KYC files, credit notes, financial statements, collateral documents, appraisal reports, sanction letters, investigation reports, audit findings, board and risk-committee notes, regulatory correspondence, legal files.

## Sensitive information

Customer identity and account data, credit scores and models, suspicious-transaction reports, fraud investigations, medical records (for insurance-adjacent products), claims evidence, actuarial models (for insurers), supervisory information, investment strategy.

## AI uses

- KYC-document extraction
- Credit-file completeness
- Financial-statement analysis
- Fraud-case chronology
- AML alert triage
- Regulatory-report drafting
- Claims-document extraction (insurance)
- Underwriting evidence search
- Policy comparison

## Constraints

- DPDP Act obligations
- RBI outsourcing and cyber-risk controls
- Payment-data localisation (where applicable)
- Model-risk management
- Explainability for credit, fraud and claims decisions
- Privileged-access controls
- Auditability
- Customer grievance and correction rights
- Retention and deletion
- Data leakage through prompts and logs

RBI requires payment-system data to be stored in India and has published a responsible-AI framework for financial-sector adoption. RBI information-security guidance expressly treats customer information and internal budgets as sensitive.

AI should remain advisory for lending, claims repudiation, fraud accusation and regulatory enforcement.
