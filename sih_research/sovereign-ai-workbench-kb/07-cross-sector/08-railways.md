- Train planning and control
- Rolling-stock maintenance
- Coach and locomotive production

- Procurement and stores

- Revenue and catering through IRCTC

## Documents

Maintenance schedules, inspection sheets, defect reports, failure memos, safety circulars, train-control logs, signal and interlocking records, coach/locomotive history, works contracts, timetables, crew and roster records, accident-investigation reports, passenger and ticketing records.

## Sensitive information

Signalling and interlocking configuration, control-room information, infrastructure weaknesses, maintenance deferments, accident investigations, passenger personal and payment data, security arrangements, pre-release train schedules.

## AI uses

- Predictive maintenance
- Wheel/axle and bearing defect analysis
- Failure chronology
- Track-inspection image analysis
- Safety-report search
- Workshop planning
- Passenger-service assistants
- Timetable and crew optimisation

## Deployment posture

Segmented railway networks and controlled government platforms. Signalling and control data require isolated treatment.
