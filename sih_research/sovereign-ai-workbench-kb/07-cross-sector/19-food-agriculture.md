# Food and Agriculture

## Organisations

FCI, NAFED, state food corporations, ICAR institutes, agricultural universities, warehousing and procurement agencies, CWC, SWC, APEDA, NDDB, Agricultural Marketing Boards, food-processing PSUs.

## Processes

Crop procurement, quality inspection, storage, stock accounting, movement and allocation, distribution, price and subsidy administration, agricultural research.

## Documents

Procurement contracts, stock registers, warehouse inspection reports, quality certificates, movement plans, beneficiary/distribution records, research data, trial results, pest and disease observations.

## Sensitive information

Procurement prices and bids, strategic stock locations, distribution plans, farmer and beneficiary data, research and breeding data, warehouse vulnerabilities.

## AI uses

- Procurement forecasting
- Stock reconciliation
- Warehouse-condition analysis
- Quality-document processing
- Scheme monitoring
- Research search
