# Technical Bid Evaluation Report

Purpose: determines whether bidders meet technical and qualification requirements. Should not rank prices unless procedure explicitly combines technical-commercial evaluation. Typical length: 10–40 pages plus clause-by-clause matrix as attachment.

## Structure

### Cover

Tender number, tender title, procurement method, department, technical evaluation committee, date, revision, confidentiality classification.

### 1.0 Requirement and scope

Background, equipment, scope of overhaul, work location, required duration, deliverables, inspection/testing, HSE requirements, warranty, qualification requirements.

### 2.0 Tender history

Tender issue date, pre-bid meeting, addenda, clarifications, bid deadline, bidders received, technical-bid opening date.

### 3.0 Bidders

| Bidder | Bid received | Eligibility documents | Technical package | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bidder A | Yes | Complete | Complete | Evaluated |

### 4.0 Preliminary responsiveness

Bid security, signed forms, authority to sign, required declarations, eligibility, conflict of interest, integrity-pact documents, required certificates, submission format.

### 5.0 Clause-by-clause compliance matrix

| Clause | Requirement | Bidder response | Compliance | Deviation | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4.2 | OEM-qualified compressor overhaul team | Team CVs attached | Comply | None | Vol. II, p. 45 |
| 5.4 | Completion within 30 days | Bidder offers 38 days | Deviate | Major | Vol. I, p. 17 |

Status conventions: C — Compliant, PC — Partially compliant, D — Deviates, NC — Non-compliant, NA — Not applicable, Clarification — Response required.

### 6.0 Technical deviations

Separate: acceptable deviations, deviations requiring clarification, deviations with cost/time impact, unacceptable deviations, deviations affecting safety, reliability or warranty.

### 7.0 Bidder capability

Similar work, OEM authorisation, experience with specific compressor, qualified manpower, tools and facilities, HSE record, quality system, references, financial/technical capacity.

### 8.0 Method statement and schedule

Disassembly, inspection, repair, balancing, testing, reassembly, commissioning, critical path, shift and manpower plan, contingency.

### 9.0 HSE and quality

HSE plan, competency, permit requirements, LOTO compliance, confined-space arrangements, inspection and test plan, non-conformance process, calibration, traceability.

### 10.0 Technical queries

| Query | Bidder | Clause | Question | Response date | Final status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### 11.0 Final technical qualification

Technically acceptable, acceptable subject to conditions, technically unacceptable, reasons.

### 12.0 Recommendation

Use controlled language:

> Bidders A and C are technically acceptable. Bidder B is not technically acceptable because it excludes rotor dynamic balancing and offers a 38-day completion period against the mandatory 30-day requirement.

Do not include undisclosed commercial rankings in the technical report.

### 13.0 Signatures

User department, technical committee, procurement, finance acknowledgement (where required), competent authority.

### 14.0 Attachments

Technical specifications, bidder submissions, compliance matrix, clarifications, qualification certificates, deviations, committee minutes, evaluation checklist.

## Good vs. bad

Good: evaluates only disclosed criteria, uses clause-level references, records every deviation, separates clarification from negotiation, preserves bidder confidentiality, explains disqualification clearly, allows audit reconstruction.

Bad: uses vague "technically suitable," introduces criteria after bid opening, mixes technical and price information improperly, omits bidder clarifications, treats silence as compliance, gives unexplained recommendation.
