# Inspection Recommendation Note

Purpose: asks management to accept a known equipment condition, impose restrictions, repair or replace equipment, or approve a monitoring plan. Typical length: 3–10 pages excluding attachments; complex pressure-vessel or pipeline case may be 15–30 pages.

## Structure

### Cover and document control

Company and refinery, department, title `Inspection Recommendation Note – [Equipment Tag]`, document number, revision, unit and area, equipment tag, date, prepared/reviewed/approved by, confidentiality and controlled-copy status.

### 1.0 Executive recommendation

State the decision requested in one paragraph:

> Continue operation of V-204 at a maximum pressure of 18 barg until 31-Dec-2026, subject to weekly UT monitoring and replacement during the next planned shutdown.

Include requested decision, immediate risk, deadline, consequence of not approving, whether operation is allowed, restricted or prohibited.

### 3.0 Defect or degradation description

Defect type, location, orientation, dimensions, whether localised or general, whether through-wall, whether active or stable, photographs and annotated drawing reference.

Example: Localised external thinning was detected at the 6 o'clock position of the outlet elbow, approximately 420 mm downstream of the vessel nozzle. Minimum measured thickness was 7.8 mm against a required minimum of 9.6 mm.

### 4.0 Inspection evidence

Inspection date, method, contractor, technician qualification, instrument ID and calibration, number of readings, reading uncertainty, previous inspection results, NDT reports, photographs, process conditions during inspection.

### 5.0 Calculation basis

Nominal thickness, current thickness, previous thickness, corrosion rate, required minimum thickness, remaining life, design pressure/temperature, operating pressure/temperature, weld efficiency, corrosion allowance, calculation method, formula and assumptions.

Long-term corrosion-rate expression:

CR = (t_previous − t_current) / Δt


State whether rate is short-term, long-term, conservative or adjusted for measurement uncertainty.

### 6.0 Applicable codes and standards

Exact edition and relevant section of: API RP 580/581 for RBI, API 579-1/ASME FFS-1 for fitness-for-service, applicable design code, OISD standard, statutory inspection requirement, internal engineering standard.

### 7.0 Current risk assessment

Probability of failure, consequence of failure, risk ranking, hazardous inventory, personnel exposure, environmental impact, production impact, existing safeguards, uncertainty.

| Risk item | Current condition | Consequence | Existing safeguard | Residual risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Local wall thinning | 7.8 mm minimum | Loss of containment | Weekly UT and reduced pressure | Medium/high |

### 8.0 Proposed action

Options: immediate shutdown, temporary repair, permanent repair, replacement, derating, additional inspection, coating or cladding, process-condition restriction, increased monitoring, no action with justification.

### 9.0 Operating restrictions

Maximum pressure, maximum temperature, maximum flow, fluid-service restriction, minimum inspection frequency, prohibited operating modes, required alarms or trips, responsible monitoring department.

### 10.0 Repair deadline and plan

Repair method, work order/project number, required materials, contractor, shutdown requirement, target date, critical-path impact, temporary measures, release-to-service conditions.

### 11.0 Monitoring requirement

Method, location, frequency, acceptance limit, data owner, escalation trigger, record system.

### 12.0 Approvals required

Inspection engineer, integrity or corrosion engineer, mechanical engineer, process engineer, operations head, HSE/process-safety representative, refinery or unit head, statutory inspector (where applicable).

### 13.0 Attachments

Inspection report, thickness table, NDT reports, photographs, marked-up isometric, P&ID, calculation sheet, FFS report, RBI extract, work order, process historian trend.

## Good vs. bad

Good: identifies exact equipment and location, separates measured facts from assumptions, shows calculations and uncertainty, states decision requested, specifies deadline and owner, links every conclusion to evidence, makes operating restrictions measurable.

Bad: says "equipment is unsafe" without quantified basis, uses outdated drawing, mixes nominal and minimum thickness, omits measurement uncertainty, recommends "monitor closely" without frequency or trigger, has no clear approval authority, treats preliminary contractor report as final FFS assessment.
