# Daily Unit Performance Report

Purpose: short operational and engineering report, usually 2–8 pages plus an Excel workbook. Some refineries use a dashboard; others use a daily Word/Excel report.

## Structure

### Header

Date, unit, reporting period, prepared by, reviewed by, data cut-off time, data-quality status.

### 1.0 Executive summary

Unit status, throughput vs. plan, main deviation, quality status, equipment constraint, required action.

### 2.0 Throughput versus plan

| Parameter | Plan | Actual | Variance | Comment |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Feed rate | 850 m³/h | 820 m³/h | -3.5% | Pump restriction |

### 3.0 Feed and product yields

Feed quantity, feed quality, product rates, yield percentages, losses, recycle, material-balance closure.

### 4.0 Energy and utilities

Fuel gas, steam, power, hydrogen, cooling water, specific energy consumption, flaring, utility constraints.

### 5.0 Product quality

Product, sample time, test, specification, actual, status, release/hold decision.

### 6.0 Equipment availability

Critical equipment, running/standby/out-of-service status, maintenance activity, bypass, constraint, expected restoration.

### 7.0 Deviations and events

Event time, description, cause/status, impact, containment, investigation reference.

### 8.0 Actions

| Action | Owner | Due date | Status |
|---|---|---|---|

### 9.0 Data sources and notes

Historian period, LIMS sample IDs, production-accounting source, EAM report, manual entries, missing values, estimation basis.

### 10.0 Approval

Process engineer, operations supervisor, unit head, planning/production manager (if required).

## Good vs. bad

Good: uses consistent reporting period, reconciles production numbers, shows units and basis, separates actual data from estimates, highlights exceptions rather than hiding them, links actions to owners.

Bad: mixes shift and calendar-day figures, uses screenshots without source tags, omits data-quality caveats, shows plan/actual without explaining variance, treats a missing lab result as product compliance.
