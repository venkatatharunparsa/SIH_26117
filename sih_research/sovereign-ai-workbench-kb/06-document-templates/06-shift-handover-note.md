# Shift Handover Note

Purpose: short operational-control document. Typical length: 1–5 pages per unit or shift, with live permit and isolation registers attached or linked.

## Structure

### Header

Site, unit, date, outgoing shift, incoming shift, outgoing engineer/operator, incoming engineer/operator, time of handover, document/logbook number.

### 1.0 Unit status

| Unit | Status | Rate | Constraint | Next action |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| CDU-1 | Running | 850 m³/h | Furnace limitation | Monitor outlet temperature |

Status codes: Running, Standby, Shutdown, Startup, Restricted, Under maintenance, Emergency.

### 2.0 Equipment status

Running equipment, standby equipment, equipment unavailable, temporary repairs, bypassed instruments, critical alarms, equipment returned to service.

### 3.0 Abnormal conditions

Event, time, current condition, action taken, risk, escalation status, incident/MOC reference.

### 4.0 Open permits

Permit number, job, location, contractor, permit type, gas-test status, expiry, issuer, receiver, required next action.

### 5.0 Isolations and LOTO

Equipment, isolation type, blind/lock number, location, permit reference, isolation owner, status, restoration restriction.

### 6.0 Open work orders

Work-order number, equipment, description, priority, maintenance status, parts status, expected completion, impact on operation.

### 7.0 Tank and inventory status

Tank, product, level, volume, quality status, inlet/outlet status, transfer plan, overfill or dispatch concern.

### 8.0 Product quality

Product, sample, result, specification, status, quarantine or release, follow-up.

### 9.0 Pending decisions

Examples: whether to increase rate, whether to start standby pump, whether to defer maintenance, whether to reroute product, whether to initiate shutdown.

### 10.0 Next-shift instructions

Action-oriented language:

- "Verify P-204B seal leakage at 22:00."
- "Maintain HCU reactor outlet temperature below 365°C."
- "Confirm repeat sulphur sample before product release."
- "Do not close PTW-2026-184 until blind B-17 is verified."

### 11.0 Handover confirmation

Outgoing signature, incoming signature, supervisor confirmation, time, comments.

## Good vs. bad

Good: current and time-stamped, focuses on what next shift must know, lists live permits and isolations, separates confirmed facts from assumptions, identifies unresolved risks, uses equipment tags and work-order numbers.

Bad: long narrative with no priorities, omits equipment unavailable, fails to list active permits, uses informal names instead of tags, copies yesterday's handover without updating status.
