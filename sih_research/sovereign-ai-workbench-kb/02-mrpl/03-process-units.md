# MRPL Process Units

The following description combines units identified in MRPL environmental and public technical documents with standard process meanings. Capacity figures are not consistently published in one current document; where a figure is available from an environmental-clearance document, it is shown as an approximate or approval-related value rather than assumed to be current nameplate capacity.

## Main process units

| Unit | What it does | Typical knowledge and data generated |
|---|---|---|
| CDU — Crude Distillation Unit | Separates crude oil into fractions such as LPG, naphtha, kerosene, diesel-range streams, gas oil and residue according to boiling point | Crude assay, operating envelopes, column temperatures, tray pressures, cut points, energy balance, daily operating reports |
| VDU — Vacuum Distillation Unit | Separates heavy atmospheric residue under vacuum so that high-boiling material can be recovered without excessive thermal cracking | Vacuum-column profiles, furnace conditions, vacuum-system performance, residue quality, yield calculations |
| NSU / naphtha stabilisation and related units | Stabilises and conditions light hydrocarbon or naphtha streams and removes unwanted light ends | Stream assays, vapour-pressure data, material balances and operating procedures |
| NHT / naphtha hydrotreating | Removes sulphur, nitrogen and other contaminants from naphtha before reforming or petrochemical use | Catalyst performance, feed contaminants, reactor conditions, hydrogen consumption and product specifications |
| CCR / catalytic reforming | Converts low-octane naphtha into high-octane reformate and generates hydrogen; also produces aromatics precursors | Catalyst-cycle data, reformer severity, octane, hydrogen balance, regeneration records |
| DHDT / diesel hydrotreating | Removes sulphur and nitrogen from diesel-range streams and improves product quality | Sulphur results, reactor temperature, hydrogen consumption, catalyst activity and product release records |
| GOHDS / gas-oil hydrodesulphurisation | Treats gas oils to meet sulphur and quality specifications | Feed/product quality, reactor conditions, catalyst life and compliance reports |
| HCU — Hydrocracker Unit | Uses hydrogen and catalyst to convert heavier streams into cleaner diesel, kerosene and lighter products | Hydrogen network data, reactor severity, catalyst performance, product yields and process constraints |
| FCC / fluid catalytic cracking | Converts heavy gas oils into lighter, higher-value products such as LPG, gasoline-range material and light olefins | Catalyst circulation, regenerator temperature, riser conditions, gasoline quality, emissions and yield optimisation |
| PFCC / petrochemical FCC | Converts selected heavy or hydroprocessed streams into propylene and other petrochemical feedstocks, with higher emphasis on olefin production | Propylene yield, catalyst activity, regenerator data, wet-gas-compressor performance, petrochemical feed quality |
| DCU — Delayed Coking Unit | Thermally cracks heavy residue into lighter vapours and petroleum coke; the process occurs in coke drums rather than in a continuously circulating catalyst bed | Coke-drum cycles, furnace severity, drum switching, coke quality, wet-gas-compressor data, shutdown and decoking records |
| VBU / bitumen unit | Produces or adjusts bitumen grades for road and industrial applications | Penetration, softening point, viscosity, grade certification and dispatch records |
| Hydrogen-generation and hydrogen-recovery facilities | Produce and recover hydrogen required by hydrotreaters and hydrocrackers | Hydrogen purity, pressure, reformer performance, fuel use and network balancing |
| Sulphur-recovery units | Convert hydrogen sulphide into elemental sulphur and reduce sulphur emissions | H₂S concentration, sulphur production, tail-gas performance and emissions data |
| Amine treating and sour-water stripping | Remove acidic gases and contaminants from process streams and wastewater | Amine quality, H₂S loading, corrosion indicators, stripper performance and wastewater parameters |
| PPU — Polypropylene Unit | Polymerises propylene into polypropylene pellets and produces different grades | Polymer-grade quality, catalyst recipes, melt-flow index, production campaigns, silo inventory and customer complaints |
| Aromatics / OMPL complex | Produces para-xylene and benzene from refinery-derived aromatics streams | Aromatics composition, crystallisation/separation performance, product purity and plant availability |

An environmental-clearance document identifies the principal configuration as including CDU/VDU, delayed coking, hydrocracker, PFCC, polypropylene, diesel-hydrotreating, naphtha/reforming and associated units. It describes the existing refinery configuration at approximately 16.6 MMTPA and an operating-hours-based expansion to 18.2 MMTPA.

MRPL has also publicly reported the implementation of advanced process control in CDU/VDU, diesel hydrotreating and PFCC units, with further implementation in isomerisation, CDU/VDU and FCC gasoline systems.

## Utilities and offsites

| System | Function |
|---|---|
| Captive power / grid power system | Supplies electrical power to pumps, compressors, furnaces, control systems and buildings |
| Steam generation and distribution | Provides process heat, stripping steam and turbine power |
| Cooling-water system | Removes heat from condensers, exchangers and process equipment |
| Desalination plant | Produces freshwater from seawater or saline sources |
| Sewage-water treatment and reuse | Treats city sewage for industrial use |
| Effluent treatment plant | Treats oily water, chemical wastewater and process effluent |
| Boiler-feedwater and demineralisation | Produces high-purity water for boilers and steam systems |
| Instrument air and plant air | Operates pneumatic control valves and plant tools |
| Nitrogen system | Provides inerting and purging for tanks, vessels and process equipment |
| Flare system | Safely disposes of hydrocarbon gases during startup, shutdown and upset conditions |
| Firewater system | Provides emergency firefighting capability |
| Waste-management systems | Handle oily sludge, spent catalysts, sulphur, coke and hazardous waste |
| Laboratory and sampling facilities | Test crude, intermediates, products, water and emissions |

MRPL reported using more than 5.6 million cubic metres of desalinated water and more than 5.2 million cubic metres of treated city sewage water in FY2024–25, while recycling about 60% of internally generated effluent.
