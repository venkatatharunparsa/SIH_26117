# MRPL Company Profile

## Overview

Mangalore Refinery and Petrochemicals Limited (MRPL) is a Schedule-A central public-sector oil-refining and petrochemicals company headquartered at Kuthethoor, near Mangaluru, Karnataka. It is a listed subsidiary of Oil and Natural Gas Corporation Limited (ONGC), with HPCL as the other major shareholder. MRPL operates a large coastal refinery, associated marine and pipeline infrastructure, polypropylene facilities, and integrated petrochemical assets in the adjacent Mangaluru Special Economic Zone.

## Ownership and group structure

MRPL was originally formed as a joint venture between HPCL and the Aditya Birla Group. ONGC acquired the Aditya Birla Group's stake in 2003 and subsequently became the controlling shareholder. MRPL has remained a CPSE since 2005 and was designated a Schedule-A CPSE in 2013.

The former ONGC Mangalore Petrochemicals Limited (OMPL) was amalgamated into MRPL with effect from 1 May 2022. The integrated aromatics facility is capable of producing approximately:

- 0.905 MMTPA of para-xylene
- 0.273 MMTPA of benzene

MRPL also has Shell-MRPL Aviation Fuel Services Limited, a joint venture between MRPL and Shell for aviation turbine fuel sales.

## FY2024–25 operating highlights

- Record crude throughput of 18.04 MMT
- 120% utilization against the 15-MMTPA reference capacity
- Best-ever distillate yield of 81.93%
- Lowest reported specific energy consumption of 70.71
- Fuel and loss reduced to 10.42%
- New crude grades processed, including Kaliningrad, Varandey and Merey-16
- New product: toluene
- Record polypropylene sales
- New cast-polypropylene film grade
- 2.99 MMT of direct domestic sales volume
- Commissioning of a Bengaluru-area marketing terminal
- Commissioning of a new bitumen train
- Commissioning of a PFCC regenerator stack wet-scrubber system

## Capacity note

Capacity figures differ by source. MoPNG reports 15 MMTPA as the present installed capacity, while an environmental-clearance document describes a 16.6-MMTPA operating configuration and an 18.2-MMTPA operating-hours expansion. These figures likely reflect different regulatory and reporting bases.
