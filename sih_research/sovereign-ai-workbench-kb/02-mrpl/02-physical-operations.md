# MRPL Physical Operations

MRPL's industrial footprint is not limited to the refinery battery limits. A useful systems view includes the following locations and asset groups:

| Location / asset group | Function |
|---|---|
| Main refinery at Kuthethoor/Katipalla | Crude distillation, secondary conversion, hydrotreating, blending, utilities and product storage |
| Coastal marine facilities near Mangaluru | Receipt of crude and dispatch or receipt of petroleum products through ship/jetty infrastructure |
| Subsea / underground pipelines | Transfer of crude and products between marine facilities, refinery and storage areas |
| Tank farms | Crude, intermediate, finished-product, chemical and utility-feed storage |
| Mangaluru SEZ aromatics complex | Para-xylene, benzene and related aromatics production |
| Polypropylene facilities | Conversion of refinery-derived propylene into polymer grades |
| Marketing terminal at Devangonthi, Bengaluru | Regional product distribution, particularly for Karnataka |
| Retail network | Fuel marketing in Karnataka, Kerala and Tamil Nadu |
| Product dispatch infrastructure | Road, rail, pipeline and marine movement of petroleum products |
| Utilities and offsites | Power, steam, cooling water, desalination, effluent treatment, flare systems, compressed air, nitrogen and wastewater management |

MRPL public documents confirm pipeline-related work between the refinery, jetty and New Mangalore Port facilities, as well as storage-tank maintenance and inspection activities.
