# MRPL AI and Digital Initiatives

There is meaningful public evidence that MRPL is already using advanced digital optimisation, although public material does not establish a complete enterprise AI strategy.

## Advanced process control

MRPL has reported implementation of advanced process control in:

- CDU/VDU-1
- Diesel hydrotreating
- PFCC
- Isomerisation
- CDU/VDU-2
- FCC gasoline-related systems

Advanced process control is especially relevant to a workbench because it produces valuable engineering knowledge: constraint definitions, manipulated-variable limits, controller performance, tuning changes, operating objectives and exception analysis.

## AI/ML and predictive or prescriptive models

MRPL's FY2024–25 chairman's speech states that the company implemented:

- Real-time predictive models in polypropylene
- Real-time predictive models in the power plant
- Real-time predictive models in the PFCC unit
- Prescriptive models in hydrotreating units

The stated objectives include improving throughput, yield and energy efficiency.

This is the strongest public evidence located for MRPL-specific AI/ML deployment. It suggests that MRPL's AI use is already moving beyond generic experimentation toward process and reliability use cases.

## Innovation Centre

The Innovation Centre reportedly works on:

- Refining process optimisation
- AI/ML systems for process and reliability
- Petrochemicals
- Plastic circularity
- Pharmaceutical synthesis
- Active pharmaceutical ingredients
- Sustainable and alternative-fuel technologies

MRPL also reported a PET-depolymerisation pilot targeted for commissioning by the end of 2025, in-house synthesis of isobutyl benzene, API-related research, and 29 patents filed with 12 granted at the time of the FY2024–25 statement.

## Environmental and energy digitisation context

MRPL's public material highlights:

- A PFCC regenerator stack wet scrubber
- Water reuse and desalination
- A grid-power infrastructure project
- A 20-KLPD sustainable aviation fuel project
- A Net Zero target year of 2038

These areas generate substantial data for an AI workbench: emissions, utility consumption, energy intensity, water balance, project controls, laboratory data and regulatory evidence.

## Tenders and public procurement

Public MRPL tender records show engineering and infrastructure procurement, including:

- Tank inspection and maintenance-related work
- Underground pipeline repairs between the refinery, jetty and port areas
- Power-system upgrades
- Industrial and security technologies

A public tender aggregator also shows an AI-based explosion-proof CCTV requirement associated with MRPL; this should be verified against the official tender portal before treating it as an established commissioned system.

## What is NOT publicly confirmed

I did not find a reliable official public source confirming:

- A specific MRPL enterprise generative-AI platform
- A named large-language-model deployment
- A fully documented digital-twin programme
- Product names for ERP, EAM, historian, LIMS or DMS
- Internal organisation chart at role level
- Departmental headcounts

That absence should not be interpreted as proof that no such work exists; industrial projects are often disclosed through tenders, internal programmes or vendor contracts rather than annual reports.

## Gap between current AI and the problem statement

| MRPL already does | Problem statement asks for |
|---|---|
| Process optimisation AI | Knowledge work AI |
| Real-time predictive models | Document understanding |
| Prescriptive models | Scanned inspection report processing |
| Advanced process control | Approval note generation |
| Condition monitoring app | Multi-step agentic workflows |
| Innovation Centre research | Air-gapped document workbench |
| 29 patents | Multi-model routing |
| Process historian analytics | Permission-aware retrieval |

This is the gap. MRPL has solved process optimisation AI. The problem statement is asking for knowledge work AI — a completely different problem.
