# MRPL Departments and Organizational Functions

MRPL's exact current organisation chart and departmental headcounts are not fully published. The following is a practical MRPL-specific functional map based on its refinery, petrochemical, marketing and CPSE responsibilities.

| Function | Typical MRPL responsibilities |
|---|---|
| Refinery operations | Run process units safely; control rates, temperatures, pressures, inventories and product quality; coordinate startups, shutdowns and abnormal operations |
| Field operations | Operate pumps, compressors, furnaces, vessels, valves, tanks and loading systems from the field; perform rounds and local isolations |
| Central control room / panel operations | Monitor DCS displays, alarms, interlocks, process constraints and unit-wide performance; execute operating changes |
| Process engineering | Prepare material and energy balances; troubleshoot yield and quality problems; optimise unit severity; support debottlenecking and advanced process control |
| Planning and economics | Select crude slates; prepare refinery production plans; balance crude cost, product demand, unit constraints, inventory and margins |
| Scheduling and supply chain | Schedule vessel receipts, tank movements, unit feed changes, product dispatch and inter-site transfers |
| Mechanical maintenance | Maintain rotating equipment, pumps, compressors, turbines, gearboxes, static equipment, piping and mechanical systems |
| Electrical | Maintain substations, switchgear, motors, transformers, cables, protection systems, UPS and emergency power |
| Instrumentation and control | Maintain transmitters, control valves, analysers, DCS, PLC, SIS, fire-and-gas systems and calibration records |
| Reliability engineering | Analyse failures, bad actors, equipment criticality, mean time between failures, vibration and condition-monitoring data |
| Inspection and integrity | Manage RBI, corrosion circuits, thickness measurements, pressure-vessel inspection, statutory certification, fitness-for-service and life extension |
| Civil and structural | Maintain roads, foundations, buildings, drainage, tank foundations, pipe racks, jetties and structural assets |
| Turnaround / shutdown management | Plan major maintenance outages, define work packs, coordinate contractors, manage critical-path activities and restart readiness |
| Projects and engineering | Develop feasibility studies, basic and detailed engineering, project estimates, procurement specifications, construction and commissioning plans |
| HSE / process safety | Manage permits, hazard studies, MOC, emergency response, fire protection, industrial hygiene, environmental monitoring and incident investigations |
| Environment | Manage emissions, effluent, water use, waste, consent conditions, environmental statements and sustainability reporting |
| Quality control / laboratory | Test crude, intermediates, fuels, bitumen, polypropylene, water, chemicals and emissions; certify products against standards |
| Production accounting | Reconcile crude receipts, tank movements, process yields, losses, product production and closing inventories |
| Procurement and contracts | Issue requisitions and tenders; evaluate vendors; manage technical-commercial bids, purchase orders, contracts and vendor performance |
| Materials management | Maintain spare-parts inventory, warehouse controls, critical spares, preservation, stock levels and materials master data |
| Marketing and sales | Sell fuels, bitumen, polypropylene, aromatics and specialty products; manage customers, pricing, dispatch and dealer networks |
| Finance and accounts | Manage budgets, capitalisation, costing, inventory valuation, treasury, taxation, statutory accounts, audits and management reporting |
| Human resources | Recruitment, industrial relations, training, performance management, payroll, welfare, succession and contractor labour compliance |
| Industrial relations and administration | Manage unions, labour agreements, township or site administration, security, transport, welfare and local liaison |
| IT | Manage ERP, networks, identity, endpoint systems, data centres, applications, service desk, cybersecurity and enterprise data |
| OT / control systems | Manage DCS, PLC, SIS, historian, industrial networks, control-system backups, patch governance and cyber segmentation |
| Legal | Handle contracts, litigation, regulatory matters, land, environmental issues, procurement disputes and legal opinions |
| Company secretary / corporate governance | Board and committee meetings, statutory filings, stock-exchange disclosures, shareholder matters and governance records |
| Corporate communications | Investor communication, press releases, website content, stakeholder communication, crisis communication and brand management |
| Corporate planning / strategy | Long-range plans, diversification, petrochemicals, sustainability, investment cases and group-level coordination |
| Internal audit and vigilance | Review controls, procurement, finance, operations, fraud risk, compliance and public-sector integrity matters |
| R&D / Innovation Centre | Develop process optimisation, AI/ML, petrochemical, circularity, pharmaceutical and alternative-fuel technologies |
| CSR and public affairs | Manage community programmes, government liaison, local development and CSR reporting |
| Security and fire services | Protect people and assets, manage access control, emergency response, firewater readiness and industrial security |

MRPL's FY2024–25 public statement specifically identifies an Innovation Centre working on refining, petrochemicals, sustainability and active pharmaceutical ingredients. It also states that MRPL had filed 29 patents, of which 12 had been granted at the time of the statement.
