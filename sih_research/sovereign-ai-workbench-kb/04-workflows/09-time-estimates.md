# Time and Effort Estimates

These are industry-based estimates, not audited sector benchmarks. "Finding/assembling" includes locating documents, extracting tables, reconciling data and preparing attachments. "Analysis" means expert interpretation and decision-making.

## Interpretation

AI will not remove the need for:

- Design authority
- Legal judgement
- Statutory approval
- Safety decisions
- Credit accountability
- Procurement committee responsibility
- Scientific validation

It reduces:

- Search
- Extraction
- Reconciliation
- First-pass comparison
- Timeline construction
- Report formatting
- Repetitive drafting
- Action-register maintenance

## Summary of AI impact

| Category | Typical AI time saving |
|---|---|
| Search and retrieval | 60–90% |
| Data extraction from PDFs/scans | 70–90% |
| Reconciliation of multi-source data | 40–60% |
| First-pass document drafting | 50–80% |
| Timeline and chronology construction | 60–80% |
| Report formatting | 60–90% |
| Approval routing and tracking | 20–40% |

## What this means for ROI

The value proposition is not replacing engineers or approvers. It is eliminating the 30–70% of effort spent on finding, extracting, reconciling and formatting evidence, so skilled professionals can focus on actual analysis, judgement and decision-making.

## Evidence limitations

- These are industry-based estimates, not audited benchmarks
- Elapsed time varies with organisational review cycles
- Actual analysis share may be higher for complex cases (FFS, grid studies)
- AI time savings depend on document quality, model accuracy and integration depth
- Public data on internal workflows is limited; estimates should be validated through customer discovery
