# Senior Manager and Approver

## Role

Senior managers do not usually need another broad document repository. They need a decision cockpit that gives them the right evidence quickly and clearly.

Typical users include:

- General managers
- Executive directors
- Chief general managers
- Refinery heads
- Functional heads
- Directors
- Board and committee secretariat personnel

## Typical day

- Review production and safety KPIs
- Attend morning coordination meetings
- Resolve cross-functional constraints
- Review investment proposals
- Approve procurement recommendations
- Review major maintenance and turnaround status
- Review incidents and corrective actions
- Review HSE, environmental and compliance status
- Read board or committee notes
- Review financial performance
- Meet vendors, regulators or government stakeholders
- Sign or route approval notes
- Prepare management presentations
- Review audit findings

## Documents reviewed

- Board notes
- Approval notes
- Capital-investment proposals
- Tender committee notes
- Technical bid evaluations
- Budget proposals
- Monthly performance reports
- Incident reports
- HAZOP and MOC summaries
- Turnaround dashboards
- Project-progress reports
- Legal opinions
- Audit reports
- Compliance certificates
- Financial statements
- Executive presentations
- Minutes and action trackers

## What takes time

- Reading long notes with incomplete summaries
- Checking whether conclusions are supported by evidence
- Comparing current proposals with previous approvals
- Finding the latest version
- Understanding commercial, technical and safety implications together
- Tracking which department has reviewed a document
- Detecting inconsistencies between the note, annexures and spreadsheets
- Requesting clarifications and waiting for revised drafts
- Preparing meeting packs
- Following up on action items

## Example

**Current task:** Approve a ₹25-crore compressor replacement. The senior manager may need to review failure history, production loss, reliability analysis, inspection findings, technical specification, vendor quotations, life-cycle cost, budget availability, HSE risk, alternative options, procurement recommendation, finance concurrence, legal or contract comments.

**AI-assisted task:** The workbench produces a two-page decision brief, a one-page financial comparison, a risk register, a list of unresolved questions and links to every source document. It must not hide adverse evidence simply to make the proposal appear stronger.

## Time estimate

| Activity | Typical elapsed time |
|---|---|
| Read executive package | 1–3 hours |
| Check annexures | 2–8 hours |
| Seek and receive clarifications | 1–5 days |
| Final decision | 0.5–2 hours |
| People involved | 8–20 people |

The manager's actual reading may take only a few hours, but elapsed time is often extended by clarification cycles.

## AI intervention

**Read-only data:**

- Approval note
- All annexures
- ERP budget
- Purchase requisition
- Tender evaluation
- Vendor quotes
- EAM failure history
- Inspection reports
- HSE review
- Legal comments
- Prior approval precedents

**Output:**

- Two-page decision brief
- Cross-document consistency report
- Unresolved-risk list
- Missing-concurrence list
- Option comparison
- Timeline and consequence analysis
- Draft approval conditions

**Human gate:**

The manager remains the decision-maker. The system should not issue approval or apply a signature.

## Persona table

| Attribute | Typical profile |
|---|---|
| Technical comfort | Variable; high domain knowledge but limited time for complex interfaces |
| Primary device | Laptop/desktop; mobile/tablet for approvals and dashboards |
| Location | Office, meetings, control room, site visits and travel |
| Shift pattern | Day-based, but available for major incidents and outages |
| AI willingness | High if the output is concise, source-backed and decision-oriented |
| Main concern | Accountability, incorrect summaries, missing risks and confidentiality |
| Best interface | Executive brief, evidence drill-down, approval workflow and mobile alerts |
